# Definition

**Technical Documentation**: The dossier compiled by the manufacturer demonstrating conformity of the product with applicable essential requirements.[^regulation-ec-765-2008]

# Legal Context
Defined in Article 2 of Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 and horizontal provisions of Decision No 768/2008/EC.
