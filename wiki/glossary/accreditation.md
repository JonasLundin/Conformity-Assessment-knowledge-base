# Definition

**Accreditation**: An attestation by a national accreditation body that a conformity assessment body meets the requirements set by harmonised standards.[^regulation-ec-765-2008]

# Legal Context
Defined in Article 2 of Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 and horizontal provisions of Decision No 768/2008/EC.
