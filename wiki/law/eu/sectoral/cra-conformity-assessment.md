# Summary

Chapter IV of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (**Cyber Resilience Act**) adapts the Decision 768/2008/EC conformity assessment modules to products with digital elements[^decision-768-2008-ec].

# Conformity Assessment Routes in CRA
- **Standard Products**: Internal control (Module A).
- **Important Class I**: Harmonised standards (Module A) or third-party assessment (Module B + C or Module H).
- **Important Class II**: Mandatory third-party conformity assessment (Module B + C or Module H).
- **Critical Products**: Mandatory European Cybersecurity Certificate or third-party assessment.
