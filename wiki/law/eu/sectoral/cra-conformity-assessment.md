# Summary

Under Chapter IV (Articles 31–33) of the Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847), conformity assessment procedures differ by product classification[^regulation-eu-2024-2847].

# Assessment Routes (Article 32)
1. **Default Products with Digital Elements (Art 32(1))**:
   - Internal control (**Module A**).
2. **Important Class I Products (Art 32(2))**:
   - Internal control (**Module A**) is permitted ONLY IF the manufacturer has applied harmonised standards, common specifications, or a European cybersecurity certification scheme in full.
   - If harmonised standards are not applied, applied in part, or do not exist, the manufacturer must use **Module B + C** (EU-type examination followed by conformity to type) or **Module H** (Full quality assurance).
3. **Important Class II Products (Art 32(3))**:
   - Third-party assessment is mandatory: **Module B + C** or **Module H**.
4. **Critical Products with Digital Elements (Art 32(4))**:
   - European cybersecurity certification scheme at assurance level at least 'substantial' or mandatory third-party assessment.
