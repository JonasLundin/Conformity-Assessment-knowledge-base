# Summary

Article 43 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act) establishes conformity assessment procedures for high-risk AI systems[^regulation-eu-2024-1689].

# Conformity Assessment Routes
1. **Annex III Points 2 to 8 (General High-Risk AI) (Art 43(2))**:
   - Conformity assessment based on **Internal Control (Annex VI)** is the general statutory default.
2. **Annex III Point 1 (Biometrics) (Art 43(1))**:
   - If harmonised standards are applied in full: Internal Control (Annex VI).
   - If harmonised standards do not exist, are not applied, or applied in part: Third-party assessment via **EU Type-Examination (Annex VII)** or **Quality Management System assessment (Annex VII)** by a notified body.
3. **Annex I Products (Safety Components) (Art 43(3))**:
   - Follows the conformity assessment procedure prescribed by the applicable sectoral New Legislative Framework instrument (e.g. MDR, Machinery, RED).
