# Summary

Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 sets the legal foundation for European standardisation, harmonised standards requests (mandates), and presumption of conformity across Union product law[^regulation-eu-1025-2012].

# Legal Mechanism
- **Standardisation Requests (Art 10)**: The European Commission requests European Standardisation Organisations (CEN, CENELEC, ETSI) to draft harmonised standards.
- **Presumption of Conformity (Art 2(1)(c))**: Publication of references to harmonised standards in the Official Journal of the European Union (OJEU) confers a legal presumption of conformity with the essential requirements they aim to cover.
