# Summary

Decision No 768/2008/EC establishes a common framework of general principles and reference provisions for drawing up Union product harmonisation legislation (the New Legislative Framework)[^decision-768-2008-ec].

# Architecture and Annexes
- **Annex I (Reference Provisions)**: Contains standard definitions, obligations of economic operators (manufacturers, authorized representatives, importers, distributors), conformity assessment body notification criteria (Articles R13–R28), and safeguard procedures.
- **Annex II (Conformity Assessment Modules)**: Provides the standard menu of conformity assessment procedures (Modules A through H1).
- **Annex III (EU Declaration of Conformity)**: Establishes the standard model structure for the EU declaration of conformity.
