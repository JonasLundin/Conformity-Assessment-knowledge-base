# Summary

Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/482 establishes the European Common Criteria-based cybersecurity certification scheme (EUCC)[^implementing-regulation-eu-2024-482].

# Timelines and Application
- **Adoption**: 31 January 2024.
- **Entry into Force**: 27 February 2024.
- **Date of Application**: **27 February 2025**.
- **Amendments**: Amended by Implementing Regulations (EU) 2024/3144 and (EU) 2025/2462 regarding transitional arrangements and evaluation facility requirements.
