# Summary

**Decision No 768/2008/EC** is the foundational constitutional instrument of the **New Legislative Framework (NLF)** within European product harmonisation law[^decision-768-2008-ec].

Enacted alongside Regulation (EC) No 765/2008, Decision 768/2008/EC provides the standardized model provisions, definitions, obligations of economic operators, safeguard mechanisms, and conformity assessment modules used across all subsequent EU product directives and regulations (including RED, MDR, Machinery Regulation, CRA, and AI Act).

# Core Principles Established in Annex I

### 1. Hierarchy of Economic Operators
- **Manufacturer**: Any natural or legal person who manufactures a product or has a product designed/manufactured, and markets that product under his name or trademark. Bears primary liability.
- **Authorized Representative**: Established in the EU, mandated by manufacturer for specific regulatory tasks.
- **Importer**: Established in the EU, places products from third countries on the Union market; must verify that conformity assessment was carried out by manufacturer.
- **Distributor**: Makes products available on the market after import/manufacture; exercises due care.

### 2. Presumption of Conformity
Products complying with **Harmonised European Standards** (referenced in the Official Journal of the European Union - OJEU) are legally presumed to conform to the essential requirements covered by those standards.

### 3. EU Declaration of Conformity (DoC)
A legally binding document drawn up by the manufacturer stating that the fulfillment of applicable essential requirements has been demonstrated.
