# Summary

In **Iceland (Ísland)**, conformity assessment operates within the European Economic Area (EEA) agreement framework, applying Regulation (EC) No 765/2008[^regulation-ec-765-2008].

# Accreditation and Notification Architecture
- **National Accreditation Body**: **Icelandic Accreditation (ISAC / Swedac cooperation)** operates under Article 4 of Regulation (EC) No 765/2008.
- **Notifying Authority**: **Fjarskiptastofa / Neytendastofa** designates and monitors notified bodies listed in NANDO[^nando-information-system].
- **EEA Scope**: Cooperation agreement with Nordic accreditation bodies.
