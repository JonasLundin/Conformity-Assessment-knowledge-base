# Summary

In **France**, conformity assessment is governed by Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 and national transposing legislation[^regulation-ec-765-2008].

# National Accreditation and Notification Architecture
- **National Accreditation Body (NAB)**: **Comité français d'accréditation (COFRAC)** operates as the sole national accreditation body under Article 4(1) of Regulation (EC) No 765/2008.
- **Notifying Authority**: **Direction générale des entreprises (DGE) / ANSSI** is responsible for setting up and carrying out the necessary procedures for the assessment and notification of conformity assessment bodies.
- **Notified Bodies Register**: Bodies designated by France are registered on the European Commission's [NANDO Database](https://ec.europa.eu/growth/tools-databases/nando/)[^nando-information-system].
- **National Context**: Designated under Code de la consommation Article L433-4.
