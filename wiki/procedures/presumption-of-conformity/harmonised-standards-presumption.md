# Summary

Under the New Legislative Framework, products manufactured in conformity with **harmonised standards** (or parts thereof) whose references have been published in the Official Journal of the European Union benefit from a legally binding **presumption of conformity** with essential requirements[^decision-768-2008-ec].

# Operational Conditions
- The standard must be developed under a formal standardisation mandate from the European Commission.
- The standard reference must be formally cited in the Official Journal (OJEU).
- The manufacturer maintains technical documentation demonstrating precise application of the cited clauses.
