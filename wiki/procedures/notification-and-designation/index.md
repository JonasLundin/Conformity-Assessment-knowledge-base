# Notification And Designation

Navigation index for Notification And Designation.

## Concepts

- [Monitoring, Suspension, and Withdrawal of Notification](monitoring-and-withdrawal.md) — Obligations of notifying authorities under Decision No 768/2008/EC Article R26 to monitor notified bodies and restrict, suspend, or withdraw notifications upon failure to meet criteria.
- [NANDO Database Listing](nando-listing.md) — Listing on the European Commission NANDO database is the formal statutory step making a notification effective across the European Union.
- [Notification Procedure](notification-procedure.md) — Procedure by which a notifying authority informs the Commission and Member States of a designated notified body via NANDO.
- [Subsidiaries and Subcontracting of Notified Bodies](subsidiaries-and-subcontracting.md) — Conditions under Decision No 768/2008/EC Article R20 under which notified bodies may subcontract specific tasks or use subsidiaries while retaining full responsibility.
