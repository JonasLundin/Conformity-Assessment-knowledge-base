# Summary

**Article 11 Presumption of Conformity for Notified Bodies** governs operational and statutory duties under the European New Legislative Framework[^decision-768-2008-ec][^regulation-ec-765-2008].

# Procedural Requirements
Article 11 of Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 establishes that a conformity assessment body accredited by a national accreditation body is presumed to meet the notification criteria.
