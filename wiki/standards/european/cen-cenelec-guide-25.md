# Summary

**CEN-CENELEC Guide 25** provides operational guidance for standardisation technical committees developing harmonised standards under European Commission mandates[^decision-768-2008-ec].

# Annex ZA/ZZ Architecture
- Mandatory informative Annex linking clauses of the European standard to the essential requirements of Union directives/regulations.
- Clear identification of normative references and exclusions.
