# Summary

**ISO/IEC 17020:2012** is an essential standard within the ISO/CASCO conformity assessment toolbox[^iso-casco-toolbox].

Requirements for the operation of various types of bodies performing inspection (Type A, B, and C inspection bodies).

# Application in EU Law
Harmonised standard referenced under Regulation (EC) No 765/2008[^regulation-ec-765-2008] for the accreditation and notification of conformity assessment bodies.
