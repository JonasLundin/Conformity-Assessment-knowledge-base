# Summary

ISO/IEC 17025:2017 specifies general requirements for the competence, impartiality and consistent operation of testing and calibration laboratories[^iso-iec-17025-2017].

# Application in Cybersecurity and ITSEFs
Under European cybersecurity certification schemes (e.g. EUCC), Information Technology Security Evaluation Facilities (ITSEFs) must be accredited against ISO/IEC 17025 to perform evaluation and testing activities.
