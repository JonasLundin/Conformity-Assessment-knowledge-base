# Summary

ISO/IEC 17065:2012 specifies requirements for bodies certifying products, processes and services, serving as the benchmark standard for product certification notified bodies under the New Legislative Framework[^iso-iec-17065-2012].

# Harmonisation and Accreditation
Conformity assessment bodies applying for notification under Union product legislation are accredited against EN ISO/IEC 17065 by their National Accreditation Body (NAB) under Regulation (EC) No 765/2008.
