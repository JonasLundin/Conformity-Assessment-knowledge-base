# Roles

Manufacturer, authorised representative, importer, distributor, conformity assessment body, notified body, notifying authority, national accreditation body, EA, market surveillance authority, the Commission.

## Concepts

- [Authorised Representative](authorised-representative.md) — Any natural or legal person established within the Union who has received a written mandate from a manufacturer to act on his behalf.
- [Conformity Assessment Body](conformity-assessment-body.md) — A body that performs conformity assessment activities including calibration, testing, certification and inspection.
- [Distributor](distributor.md) — Any natural or legal person in the supply chain, other than the manufacturer or importer, who makes a product available on the market.
- [European co-operation for Accreditation (EA)](european-cooperation-for-accreditation.md) — The body recognised under Article 14 of Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 that manages the European accreditation peer-evaluation infrastructure.
- [Importer](importer.md) — Any natural or legal person established within the Union who places a product from a third country on the Union market.
- [Manufacturer](manufacturer.md) — Any natural or legal person who manufactures a product or has a product designed or manufactured, and markets that product under his name or trademark.
- [Market Surveillance Authority](market-surveillance-authority.md) — An authority of a Member State responsible for carrying out market surveillance on its territory.
- [National Accreditation Body](national-accreditation-body.md) — The sole body in a Member State that performs accreditation with authority derived from the State.
- [Notified Body](notified-body.md) — A conformity assessment body officially designated by a Member State and notified to the Commission to carry out third-party tasks under Union legislation.
- [Notifying Authority](notifying-authority.md) — The national authority responsible for setting up and carrying out the procedures necessary for the assessment and notification of conformity assessment bodies.
