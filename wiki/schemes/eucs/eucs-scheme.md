# Summary

**European Cybersecurity Certification Scheme for Cloud Services (EUCS)** under Regulation (EU) 2019/881 (Cybersecurity Act)[^regulation-eu-2019-881].

Draft European cybersecurity certification scheme for cloud services developed under the Cybersecurity Act.

# Structure and Assurance Levels
Establishes Union-wide cybersecurity certification mechanisms for ICT products, services, and processes with assurance levels 'basic', 'substantial', and 'high'.
