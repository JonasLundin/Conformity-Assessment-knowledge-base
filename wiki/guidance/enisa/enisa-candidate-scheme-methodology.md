# Summary

Under the Cybersecurity Act (Regulation (EU) 2019/881), **ENISA** develops candidate European cybersecurity certification schemes following a structured, multi-stakeholder methodology[^decision-768-2008-ec].

# Scheme Development Cycle
1. Formal request from the European Commission or European Cybersecurity Certification Group (ECCG).
2. Ad-hoc stakeholder working groups and technical drafting.
3. Public consultation and delivery of the candidate scheme to the Commission for adoption as an implementing act.
