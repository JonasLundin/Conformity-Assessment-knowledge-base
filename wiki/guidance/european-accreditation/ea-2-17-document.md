# Summary

**EA-2/17 M** sets mandatory criteria for European National Accreditation Bodies (NABs) assessing conformity assessment bodies seeking notification under EU directives and regulations[^decision-768-2008-ec].

# Key Provisions
- Harmonises applicable ISO/IEC standards (e.g., ISO/IEC 17065, 17020, 17025) per module.
- Assesses competence in legal requirements, harmonised standards, and European sectoral schemes.
