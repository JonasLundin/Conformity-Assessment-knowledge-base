# Summary

**Coordination Groups of Notified Bodies** (CNBs) are established pursuant to Decision 768/2008/EC Article R30 to ensure uniform technical interpretation and mutual consistency among notified bodies[^decision-768-2008-ec].

# Administrative Deliverables
- **Recommendations for Use (RfU)**: Agreed interpretations endorsed by the Commission and Member States.
- Technical sheets addressing edge cases in testing, inspection, and auditing.
