# Summary

**Module D (Conformity to type based on quality assurance of the production process)** ensures conformity to the approved EC-type through an approved quality system for production, final product inspection and testing[^decision-768-2008-ec].

# Normative Structure
- Manufacturer operates an approved quality system (typically ISO 9001 aligned) for production, final product inspection and testing.
- Notified body audits and evaluates the quality system, carrying out periodic surveillance audits.
