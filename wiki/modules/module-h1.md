# Summary

**Module H1 (Conformity based on full quality assurance plus design examination)** supplements Module H by requiring a notified body examination of the product design and issuance of an EU design examination certificate[^decision-768-2008-ec].

# Design Examination Procedure
In addition to full quality assurance under Module H, the manufacturer submits an application for design examination to the notified body, which evaluates the technical design and issues an EU design examination certificate.
