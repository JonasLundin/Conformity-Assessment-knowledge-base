# Summary

**Module F (Conformity to type based on product verification)** is the procedure whereby a notified body carries out examinations and tests to verify conformity of products with the approved type[^decision-768-2008-ec].

# Verification Options
1. **Verification of conformity of each product**: Every product is individually examined and tested.
2. **Statistical verification**: Notified body takes random samples from batches and carries out statistical examination.
