# Summary

**Module D1: Quality Assurance of the Production Process** under Annex II of Decision No 768/2008/EC[^decision-768-2008-ec].

Standalone module: manufacturer operates an approved quality system for production, final product inspection and testing without prior Module B.

# Structure and Obligations

Quality assurance of the production process without prior type examination: manufacturer compiles technical documentation and operates an approved quality system assessed by a notified body.

### Manufacturer obligations
- Draw up technical documentation.
- Ensure manufacturing process maintains conformity.
- Affix CE marking and draw up EU declaration of conformity.

### Notified body role
- Independent third-party assessment where specified by the module.
