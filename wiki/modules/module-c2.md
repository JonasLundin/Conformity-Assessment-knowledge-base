# Summary

**Module C2 (Conformity to type based on internal production control plus supervised product checks at random intervals)** supplements Module C with random finished-product checks by a notified body[^decision-768-2008-ec].

# Normative Procedure
A notified body chosen by the manufacturer carries out product checks at random intervals to verify the conformity of manufactured products with the approved EC-type.
