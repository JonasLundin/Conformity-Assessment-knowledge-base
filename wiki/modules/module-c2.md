# Summary

**Module C2: Conformity to Type Based on Internal Production Control plus Supervised Product Checks at Random Intervals** under Annex II of Decision No 768/2008/EC[^decision-768-2008-ec].

Conformity to type combined with random product checks carried out by a notified body.

# Structure and Obligations

A notified body chosen by the manufacturer carries out product checks at random intervals to verify the quality of internal production control and conformity to type.

### Manufacturer obligations
- Draw up technical documentation.
- Ensure manufacturing process maintains conformity.
- Affix CE marking and draw up EU declaration of conformity.

### Notified body role
- Independent third-party assessment where specified by the module.
