# Modules

The conformity assessment modules of Decision 768/2008/EC Annex II, one page per module, with the sector variants that reference them.

## Concepts

- [Module A1: Internal Production Control plus Supervised Product Testing](module-a1.md) — Internal production control combined with product tests on specific aspects carried out by or under responsibility of a notified body.
- [Module C1: Conformity to Type Based on Internal Production Control plus Supervised Product Testing](module-c1.md) — Conformity to type combined with product tests carried out by or under responsibility of a notified body.
- [Module D1: Quality Assurance of the Production Process](module-d1.md) — Standalone module: manufacturer operates an approved quality system for production, final product inspection and testing without prior Module B.
- [Module E1: Quality Assurance of Final Product Inspection and Testing](module-e1.md) — Standalone module: technical documentation plus approved quality system for final product inspection and testing.
- [Module F1: Conformity Based on Product Verification](module-f1.md) — Standalone module: notified body carries out examinations and tests to verify conformity of products with legislative requirements.
- [Module H1: Full Quality Assurance plus Design Examination](module-h1.md) — Highest-rigour conformity assessment procedure adding mandatory design examination and special surveillance to Module H.
- [Module A2: Internal Production Control plus Supervised Product Checks at Random Intervals](module-a2.md) — Internal production control combined with product checks at random intervals carried out by a notified body.
- [Module C2: Conformity to Type Based on Internal Production Control plus Supervised Product Checks at Random Intervals](module-c2.md) — Conformity to type combined with random product checks carried out by a notified body.
- [Module A: Internal Production Control](module-a.md) — Conformity assessment procedure whereby the manufacturer ensures and declares compliance without notified body intervention.
- [Module B: EU-Type Examination](module-b.md) — Conformity assessment phase where a notified body examines the technical design of a product and issues an EU-type examination certificate.
- [Module C: Conformity to Type Based on Internal Production Control](module-c.md) — Production-phase procedure where manufacturer ensures manufactured products conform to the type approved under Module B.
- [Module D: Conformity to Type Based on Quality Assurance of the Production Process](module-d.md) — Production-phase procedure where a notified body audits and approves the manufacturer's production quality management system (ISO 9001 adapted).
- [Module E: Conformity to Type Based on Product Quality Assurance](module-e.md) — Manufacturer operates an approved quality system for final product inspection and testing; notified body audits quality system.
- [Module F: Conformity to Type Based on Product Verification](module-f.md) — Notified body carries out examinations and tests on every product or statistical basis to verify conformity to type.
- [Module G: Conformity Based on Unit Verification](module-g.md) — Notified body examines individual product, verifies conformity with legislative requirements, and issues certificate of conformity.
- [Module H: Full Quality Assurance](module-h.md) — Comprehensive conformity assessment based on full quality assurance covering design, manufacturing, final inspection, and testing.
