# Conformity Assessment Modules

Menu of conformity assessment procedures set out in Annex II to Decision No 768/2008/EC.

## Concepts

- [Module A: Internal Production Control](module-a.md) — Manufacturer internal conformity procedure without third-party intervention.
- [Module A1: Internal Production Control plus Supervised Product Testing](module-a1.md) — Internal control supplemented by product tests carried out by or on behalf of a notified body.
- [Module A2: Internal Production Control plus Supervised Product Checks at Random Intervals](module-a2.md) — Internal control supplemented by product checks carried out at random intervals by a notified body.
- [Module B: EC-Type Examination](module-b.md) — Notified body examines technical design and verifies product specimen.
- [Module C: Conformity to Type based on Internal Production Control](module-c.md) — Manufacturer ensures products conform to the type described in the EC-type examination certificate.
- [Module C1: Conformity to Type based on Internal Production Control plus Supervised Product Testing](module-c1.md) — Conformity to type with product tests performed on behalf of the manufacturer.
- [Module C2: Conformity to Type based on Internal Production Control plus Supervised Product Checks at Random Intervals](module-c2.md) — Conformity to type supplemented by random product checks by a notified body.
- [Module D: Conformity to Type based on Quality Assurance of the Production Process](module-d.md) — Production quality assurance audited and approved by a notified body.
- [Module D1: Quality Assurance of the Production Process](module-d1.md) — Production quality assurance without prior Module B type examination.
- [Module E: Conformity to Type based on Product Quality Assurance](module-e.md) — Product quality assurance (final inspection and testing) audited by a notified body.
- [Module E1: Quality Assurance of Final Product Inspection and Testing](module-e1.md) — Final inspection quality assurance without prior Module B type examination.
- [Module F: Conformity to Type based on Product Verification](module-f.md) — Notified body carries out examinations and tests on every product or statistical batches.
- [Module F1: Conformity based on Product Verification](module-f1.md) — Product verification directly against technical documentation without prior Module B.
- [Module G: Conformity based on Unit Verification](module-g.md) — Notified body verifies individual bespoke product against technical documentation.
- [Module H: Conformity based on Full Quality Assurance](module-h.md) — Full quality assurance (design, manufacture, inspection) audited by a notified body.
- [Module H1: Conformity based on Full Quality Assurance plus Design Examination](module-h1.md) — Full quality assurance supplemented by notified body design examination.
