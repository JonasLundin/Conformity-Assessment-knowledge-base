# Summary

**Module E (Conformity to type based on product quality assurance)** ensures conformity to the approved EC-type through an approved quality system focused on final product inspection and testing[^decision-768-2008-ec].

# Normative Structure
- Manufacturer operates an approved quality system for final product inspection and testing.
- Excludes the production process itself from the quality system scope.
