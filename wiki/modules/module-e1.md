# Summary

**Module E1 (Quality assurance of final product inspection and testing)** allows application of product quality assurance directly against technical documentation without prior Module B examination[^decision-768-2008-ec].

# Normative Structure
Manufacturer establishes technical documentation and operates an approved quality system for final product inspection and testing, assessed and monitored by a notified body.
