# Summary

**Module H (Conformity based on full quality assurance)** is the conformity assessment procedure whereby the manufacturer operates an approved quality system for design, manufacture, final product inspection and testing of the products concerned[^decision-768-2008-ec].

# Quality System Requirements
- Covers design, production, final inspection and testing (aligned with ISO 9001 and ISO/IEC 27001).
- Notified body audits the quality system, including design controls, and conducts ongoing surveillance.
- Manufacturer affixes CE marking and notified body identification number.
