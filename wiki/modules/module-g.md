# Summary

**Module G (Conformity based on unit verification)** is the conformity assessment procedure whereby the notified body verifies that an individual unit of a product satisfies the legislative requirements[^decision-768-2008-ec].

# Normative Procedure
The notified body examines the technical documentation and carries out appropriate examinations and tests on each individual bespoke product unit before issuing a certificate of conformity.
