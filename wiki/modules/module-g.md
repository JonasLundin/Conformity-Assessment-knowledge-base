# Summary

**Module G: Conformity Based on Unit Verification** under Annex II of Decision No 768/2008/EC[^decision-768-2008-ec].

Notified body examines individual product, verifies conformity with legislative requirements, and issues certificate of conformity.

# Structure and Obligations

Conformity based on unit verification is the procedure whereby the manufacturer fulfils obligations and ensures and declares that the product concerned, which has been issued a certificate of conformity by a notified body, satisfies requirements.

### Manufacturer obligations
- Draw up technical documentation.
- Ensure manufacturing process maintains conformity.
- Affix CE marking and draw up EU declaration of conformity.

### Notified body role
- Independent third-party assessment where specified by the module.
