# Summary

**Module B (EC-type examination)** is that part of a conformity assessment procedure in which a notified body examines the technical design of a product and verifies and attests that the technical design meets the legislative requirements[^decision-768-2008-ec].

# Examination Types (Point 2)
1. **Production type**: Examination of a specimen, representative of the production envisaged, of the complete product.
2. **Combination type**: Assessment of the adequacy of the technical design through examination of technical documentation and supporting evidence, plus examination of specimens of critical parts.
3. **Design type**: Assessment of the adequacy of the technical design through examination of technical documentation and supporting evidence, without examination of a specimen.

# Certificate Issuance and Validity
- If the type meets legislative requirements, the notified body issues an **EC-type examination certificate** to the manufacturer.
- *Note on Validity*: Decision No 768/2008/EC Annex II Module B does not prescribe a blanket 5-year expiry; validity periods or reassessment triggers are established by sector-specific Union acts where applicable.
- **Sectoral Links**: Under the AI Act, Article 43(1) prescribes third-party assessment for biometric systems (Annex III point 1) via EU type-examination (Annex VII) or full quality assurance.
